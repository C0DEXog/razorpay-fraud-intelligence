Rather than telling the compliance officer 'the model said no', the SHAP Waterfall Plot decomposes the decision:
- The base probability starts at normal background risk.
- Then, 'burst_6' (a sudden spike of 6 transactions in 1 hour) pushes the risk score up by +0.31.
- 'is_night' (transaction executed at 2:30 AM IST) pushes it up another +0.20.
- And 'amount_round100' (a round-number amount characteristic of card testing) adds further risk weight.

This creates an instant, tamper-proof Escalation Audit Log that risk analysts can use immediately to justify freezing a payout or triggering Step-Up 2-Factor Authentication."
